title: Product Vision
treatment: manifesto
atmosphere: vision
summary: Every patient feels like the practice's only patient.
argument: |
  Making every patient feel like the only patient is a coordination problem at scale. PJM is fundamentally about reasoning across context — synthesizing each patient's situation, deciding what should happen next, handling the messy cases that don't fit a clean rule. No human can do this on 50+ patients a day. Rules engines fall apart on edge cases. **Navigate is the agentic platform that makes this possible** — AI agents reasoning at scale, where no human can on their own.

  Customers are buying AI now. Many have dedicated AI budgets. A growing number won't implement new technology unless it's AI-enabled. Without a credible AI story, we lose deals to competitors regardless of whether their AI is actually good. The window won't stay open — capability, customer expectations, and competitive pressure are all moving fast. Every month we delay is a month a competitor accumulates relationships, data, and positioning we can't easily reclaim.

  A credible AI story also keeps us at the center of the customer relationship. Our model is to partner long term, painting a vision customers want to help realize. That vision is what builds gravity and keeps competitors out. AI is table stakes for any vision in healthcare software now — without it, the broader vision falls flat.
evidence: |
  We have the foundations and the customers. No AI products in market yet, but we have what's needed for a differentiated AI-enabled product: customer relationships, workflow gravity, patient data, journey definition work in flight, and years of curated specialty knowledge from the education business. We also have the existing customer base to deploy it into. The work ahead is shipping.

  The platform provides the journey context, configuration, knowledge, and infrastructure that lets agents do real work — not generic chat, but coordinated, grounded action inside the workflows clinics already run.
