summary: |
  Existing processes carry the strategy. AI bets go through Greenlight (each must show strategy alignment) and PI Planning (competing against other work). We track AI bets specifically with a goal of shipping at least one AI bet per quarter.
argument: |
  Our existing product processes cover this — we don't need a parallel AI process, we need AI work to compete and ship inside the system we already run.

  **Greenlight bets.** AI-related bets go through the standard Greenlight process. Each bet must reference this strategy and show alignment with it. Bets that don't advance the strategy don't get approved.

  **PI Planning.** AI-related bets go through the standard PI Planning process and compete for capacity against other strategic and incremental work. We track AI-related bets specifically, with a goal of shipping at least one AI bet every quarter.
phases:
  - label: Phase 1
    title: Agent PoCs
    body: |
      Build PoCs of multiple agents to prove that our patient journey data, practice configuration, and specialty clinical knowledge can produce differentiated experiences. We don't need to PoC everything before moving to Phase 2 — architecture unlocks once we have enough understanding of the requirements. Build sequence per agent: instructions only → clinic-specific files → patient context → build from there.
  - label: Phase 2
    title: Production architecture planning
    body: |
      Once we have a few PoCs in flight, take stock of what's worth building as shared production architecture vs. what can stay agent-specific. Make a plan. Pressure-test the plan with the team and customers before committing engineering capacity.
    itemsHeading: Questions to answer
    items:
      - What did the PoCs teach us about what agents actually need from shared infrastructure?
      - Which Foundations capabilities (data model, shared substrate, configuration, knowledge layer, context assembly, model abstraction, evals) need to be built up front, and which can be deferred?
      - What's the right sequence, given that we want agents in customers' hands as fast as possible?
    output: |
      A production architecture plan that names what we'll build, in what order, and why — grounded in PoC learnings rather than guesses.
