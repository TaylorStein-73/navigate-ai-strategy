title: Stakeholders
treatment: triptych
atmosphere: who
summary: |
  AI serves three stakeholders differently. For staff, it does the work they avoid or never reach. For patients, it personalizes journeys at scale. For administrators, it surfaces patterns across patients and outcomes that drive operational redesign.
argument: |
  There are three primary stakeholders in any patient journey: clinic staff, patients, and clinic administration. AI should be directed at each in different ways — not as a single feature, but as distinct workloads with distinct success criteria.
panels:
  - label: For staff
    title: AI does the work they don't want to do or never get to.
    body: |
      Busy work like tracking, chasing, and cross-referencing. And the proactive work that never happens because nobody has time — catching stalled patients, monitoring readiness, identifying risks early. Staff get freed up for the higher-judgment work only humans can do.
  - label: For patients
    title: Focus on hyper-personalization.
    body: |
      Hyper-personalization at scale is something AI can do that nothing else can, and it's where AI most clearly improves the patient experience. This is where we focus our patient-facing AI efforts — adapting the journey to each person's situation, preferences, and pace.
  - label: For administrators
    title: Surface insights that improve journeys and outcomes.
    body: |
      AI can synthesize patterns across patients, stages, and outcomes that no human can see at scale — where journeys are leaking, which interventions work, what's driving good and bad outcomes. Administrators use these insights to redesign journeys, reallocate resources, and improve performance over time.
evidence: |
  Each stakeholder lens already has named agents in the example portfolio. For staff: daily rounds, messaging response, nurture, intake triage, ambient scribe. For patients: experience personalization, patient Q&A. For administrators: the insight agent. The portfolio will evolve from PoCs and customer feedback, but the three-lens structure is durable.
