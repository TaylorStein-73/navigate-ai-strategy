title: Our Differentiators
treatment: stacked-moats
atmosphere: differentiators
summary: |
  Five differentiators compound: agents run inside existing staff workflows, reason against a structured patient journey, draw on curated specialty knowledge, learn each customer's playbook, and act as a coordinated team. Each makes the next harder to copy.
argument: |
  What makes our agents differentiated isn't any single capability — it's that five distinct moats compound into one. A competitor matching any one of them is plausible. Matching all five, in the right order, is not.
moats:
  - title: Our agents run where staff already work.
    body: |
      Navigate has workflow gravity — staff live in it every day. Our agents show up in those existing workflows rather than asking staff to go somewhere new. The more workflow gravity we have, the more places agents show up, and the harder it is for a competitor to wedge in.
  - title: Our agents reason against a structured patient journey.
    body: |
      We are the system of record for the patient journey — the journey itself is defined here, and data about each patient's progress lives here. AI can't manage a patient journey without this. Competitors with only design tools or only execution surfaces don't have it. Customers stitching together separate vendors can't get there either.
  - title: Our agents are grounded in deep specialty knowledge.
    body: |
      Years of building patient education content across the specialties we serve gives us the clinical subject matter expertise the AI needs. EngagedMD curates this knowledge, and every customer benefits from it. A competitor starting fresh has to acquire this expertise before their AI can be useful in any specialty.
  - title: Our agents are tailored to each customer.
    body: |
      Every practice teaches the AI everything about how they operate — protocols, SOPs, communication style, escalation policies, edge cases. The AI doesn't run on generic settings. It runs on the customer's playbook. Outcome data refines it further over time. A competitor can copy features. They can't copy a customer's tailored AI.
  - title: Our agents work as a team.
    body: |
      The rounding agent surfaces a stalling patient. The nurture agent uses that signal to draft outreach. The messaging agent learns from how that patient responds. The insight agent spots patterns across all of these interactions. Each agent makes the others smarter, and the whole system reasons about the patient together rather than in isolation. A standalone agent is replicable. A coordinated team of agents grounded in shared journey context is not.
evidence: |
  These differentiators compound rather than stand alone. Workflow gravity only matters if agents have something to reason against — the structured journey. The structured journey only produces useful outputs if it's grounded in real specialty knowledge. Specialty knowledge only fits each customer if it's shaped by their playbook. And each customer's tailored AI only multiplies in value when the agents coordinate as a team. A competitor would have to replicate the whole stack, not any single piece, to match the experience.
